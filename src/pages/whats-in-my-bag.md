<p class="drop-cap">Here’s my full kit. Everything fits in the bag, except the tripod + head and the Leica M9-P with a lens, usually the 50mm, attached, which I wear across 1 shoulder, along with the WhiBal card and a press ID.</p>

* Billingham Hadley Small camera bag in khaki Fibrenyte waterproof nylon fabric
* Billingham luggage tally &amp; Billingham SP-40 shoulder pad
* Leica M3 (double-stroke) camera body, <span class="ord">No.</span> 873xxx, mfr. ca. 1957
* Charger, cable, &amp; spare battery for Leica M9-P (automobile adapter is in my MINI’s glovebox)
* Leica Tele-Elmarit-M 90mm f/2.8 telephoto lens (version II “thin” mfr. 1974 – 1990)
* Zeiss Biogon T* 35mm f/2 ZM lens
* Zeiss Sonnar T* 50mm f/1.5 ZM lens
* Small &amp; medium Micro Lens pouches
* Leica 21mm and 35mm auxiliary viewfinders
* Pocket-sized WhiBal card + stand &amp; lanyard
* Press ID
* Leica E39 UV filter
* Hoya filter kit N<sup>o</sup><sup>.</sup>HKDG46 w/ pouch, circular polarizer, ND8, &amp; UV filter
* Hoya ND2 &amp; ND4 neutral density filters
* Hoya K2 yellow filter for B+W film
* 39mm – 46mm &amp; 43mm– 46mm step-up rings
* Cowboy Studios 3-way hot shoe mountable spirit level
* Lowepro memory card carrier w/ spare SD card(s)
* Photographic Solutions cleaning kit
* LensPen lens cleaning pen &amp; Filter Klear filter cleaning pen
* Moleskine notebook w/ <cite>[The Photographer’s Rights](https://www.krages.com/ThePhotographersRight.pdf){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</cite> tucked in back pocket
* Tools for Gitzo tripod &amp; Arca-Swiss QR plate
* Sekonic L-308S light meter w/ quick reference card, instruction booklet, case &amp; strap
* MOO [business cards](https://www.moo.com/us/accessories/business-card-holders/leather){target=_blank rel="external noopener"} in leather &amp; brushed steel case
