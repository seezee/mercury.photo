It didn’t really sound like my kind of thing, but then again I happened to be home that weekend so I didn’t have a good excuse not to at least go to the audition.

  <footer>
  
  &mdash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;Rebecca Loebe, on being asked to audition for the first season of NBC’s <cite>The Voice</cite>
  </footer>
</blockquote>

As singer-songwriter <span class="h-card p-name">Rebecca Loebe</span> told the audience at the Crystal Theatre this past <time datetime="2013-07-12">July</time>, she didn’t mean to become a best-selling artist. But with over 35,000 downloads of her single (her [audition song](https://www.reverbnation.com/rebeccaloebe){target=_blank rel="external noopener"} for NBC’s <cite>The Voice</cite>, a cover of Nirvana’s <cite class="short-work">[Come As You Are](https://music.apple.com/us/album/come-as-you-are-voice-performance/id432645852?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</cite>) on iTunes, she’s an unqualified success. Already a touring musician with two EPs and two LPs under her belt, the Austin-based performer so impressed the show’s celebrity coaches <span class="h-card p-name">Adam Levine</span> and <span class="h-card p-name">Christina Aguilera</span> that they both offered to mentor her for the competition.

Even before that, she was impressing audiences and critics, garnering a win in the Grassy Hill New Folk Songwriting Competition at the <span class="h-card p-org">Kerrville Folk Festival</span>. As if that wasn’t enough, her second full-length release spent nine weeks in the Top 40 of the Americana Chart and eight weeks in the Top 10 of the Euro Americana Chart before it was recognized in the Americana Music Association’s <cite>List of the Top 100 Albums of 2010</cite>.

She’s also very funny&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&mdash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;we heard a lot of laughter in the <span class="h-card p-org">Crystal Theatre</span> audience, both at her song introductions and interjections and at her delivery of certain droll lyrics. <cite>[Thanksgiving](https://music.apple.com/us/album/thanksgiving/id253875893?i=253876092&uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</cite>, a warning to potential suitors, made us put our camera down because we were laughing so hard. This was Loebe’s first appearance at the festival.
