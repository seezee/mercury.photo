[NORMAN, Okla., 2 November 2012]&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&mdash;{.no-drop-cap}

Cleveland County voters may cast their ballots before <time datetime="2012-11-06">Tuesday</time> at the <span class="h-card p-org">Cleveland County Election Board</span>, 641 East Robinson, just east of the county fairgrounds. Voting is open until <time datetime="2012-11-02T:18:00-6:00"><span class="no-wrap">6 p.m.</span> today</time>, then again from <time datetime="2012-11-03T08:00-6:00"><span class="no-wrap">8 a.m.</span></time>&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&ndash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;<time datetime="2012-11-03T13:00-6:00"><span class="no-wrap">1 p.m.</span></time> <time datetime="2012-11-03">tomorrow (Saturday)</time>, and again from <time datetime="2012-11-05T08:00-6:00"><span class="no-wrap">8 a.m.</span></time>&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&ndash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;<time datetime="2012-11-05T18:00-6:00"><span class="no-wrap">6 p.m.</span></time> <time datetime="2012-11-05">Monday, 5 November</time>.

<mpb-dialog-img>

{% image "./src/assets/images/blog/2012-11-02-early-voting/czahller-20121102-11-41-04.jpg", "img-constrained", "A young woman with long dark hair completes a form on a clipboard before taking her seat. When her number is called, she will be called to join the line to receive her ballot.", "The Line (V) (Young Voter)" %}</mpb-dialog-img>

When we arrived to vote at <time datetime="2012-11-02T10:30-6:00">10:30 a.m. today</time>, the waiting time was about seventy-five minutes. Voters will need to show a state- or federally-issued I.D. or a voter registration card; citizens without required I.D. will have the opportunity to obtain a temporary I.D. at the main counter downstairs.
