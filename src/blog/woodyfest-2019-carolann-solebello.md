Red Molly co-founder <span class="h-card p-name">Carolann Solebello</span> performed an intimate solo set on the <span class="h-card p-org">Crystal Theatre Stage</span> during Woodyfest <time datetime="2019-07-12T11:00:00-5:00">2019</time>. The small audience attending the <time datetime="2019-07-12T11:00:00-5:00">mid-morning</time> performance were treated to songs that blended rural folk traditions with Solebello’s distinctly urban rhythmic sensibility, peppered in between with pithy, humorous anecdotes.

Since the dissolution of Red Molly, Solebello has alternated between solo performance and touring with modern folk quartet <span class="h-card p-org">No Fuss and Feathers</span>. She is a member of New York City’s <span class="h-card p-org"><span class="h-card p-name">Jack Hardy</span> Songwriters’ Exchange</span> and the winner of numerous musical awards, including first-place at the <time datetime="2011">2011</time> <span class="h-card p-org">Susquehanna Music and Arts Festival</span> (Maryland) and the <time datetime="2015">2015</time> <span class="h-card p-org">Musicians on a Mission Songwriting Contest</span> (New Jersey).

Solebello's most recent album, <cite>Shiver</cite>, [is out now](https://geo.music.apple.com/it/album/shiver/1354766373?mt=1&app=music&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}.
