<span class="h-card p-org">[Kali Ra](https://officialkalira.com){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</span> front man <span class="h-card p-name">David Goad</span> dropped by for an [interview](/blog/kali-ra-interview-pt-1) <time datetime="2012-11-15">yesterday</time> before heading over to <span class="h-card p-org">OPOLIS</span> to open for <span class="h-card p-org">CHUD</span>. After recording the interview, Mercury Photo Bureau headed downtown to photograph the show, but not before receiving an invitation from [Psychic Milk](/blog/psychic-milk-interview-gallery/)’s <span class="h-card p-name">Justin Hogan</span>[^1] to attend the same show&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&mdash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;the <a href="">[Kamals](/blog/kamals-interview-gallery/), also opening for CHUD, had had to cancel due to a medical emergency. Psychic Milk would be subbing for them.

[^1]: Hogan also plays guitar for Kali Ra.

Arriving at the venue an hour after the doors were to have officially opened, we found the place dark and the doors locked. We wandered over to the adjacent hookah bar, thinking that perhaps OPOLIS had moved and we’d missed it. We were the second party with the same question, and the bar manager assured us that OPOLIS was still located next door, and that he’d seen a van unloading equipment around back.

<mpb-dialog-img>

{% image "./src/assets/images/blog/2012-11-16-concert-was-not/czahller-20121115-22-07-00.jpg", "img-constrained", "Brian Daniel purses his lips and “threatens” to kiss Justin Hogan. Hogan’s facial expression is one of mock distress. The image is black-and-white.", "Smoochy, Smoochy" %}</mpb-dialog-img>

Strolling to the back we ran into Hogan, who told us that CHUD had bailed&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&mdash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;also for a medical emergency&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&mdash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;so OPOLIS had canceled the show. In the meantime, a video crew from <abbr title="University of Central Oklahoma">UCO</abbr> helmed by <span class="h-card p-name">Zac Haigh</span> were interviewing Kali Ra bassist <span class="h-card p-name">Brian Daniel</span> for a documentary about the band to be released next year.

We snapped a photo of Daniel and Hogan while the video crew paused to let a locomotive roll by. After that, Goad arrived with his wife, <span class="h-card  p-name p-given-name">Alicia</span>, who had been present for the interview earlier that evening. He apologized profusely for having brought us on a wild goose chase, even though it wasn’t his fault. Goad felt very bad about the prospect of other fans arriving to find a canceled show, and thought perhaps he could Skype a performance to them later that evening.

We suggested leaving a note on OPOLIS’s door saying as much, so we walked to the Bureau’s MINI Cooper to grab a Moleskine notebook. After leaving a note to [follow Goad](https://www.facebook.com/davidgoadmusik){target=_blank rel="external noopener"} on Facebook for news about the Skype concert, we retired to the <span class="h-card p-org">Abner Ale House</span>, where we debated large philosophical questions, talked of trivial things, and consumed beer.
