<span class="h-card p-name">Brad Lauretti</span> has been wandering. Originally from Brooklyn, then based in Florida,[^1] now calling Nashville home, Lauretti rarely sleeps in his own bed. The frontman for <span class="h-card p-org">This Frontier Needs Heroes</span> tours constantly: in the last three years he’s played 226 shows, ranging from one end of the continental United States to the other along with much of western Europe. The venues range in size from small dive bars to mid-sized clubs to festival stages.

[^1]: Lauretti was based in Jacksonville, where he founded the <a href="http://www.stetsonkennedy.com/residency.html" target="_blank" rel="external noopener">Stetson Kennedy Songwriter Residency</a>. A partnership with the <span class="h-card p-org">Stetson Kennedy Foundation</span>, <span class="h-card p-org">St. Johns County Parks and Recreation Department</span>, and <span class="h-card p-org">Jacksonville Songwriters Residency</span>, the program counts as alumnus Woodyfest regular <span class="h-card p-name">[Tim Easton](/blog/tim-easton-exposition-interview-gallery/)</span>.

No matter the room size, Lauretti always brings a mix of existential isolation and earnest indignation at the world’s injustices. If you’re worried that’s too “sad sack” for your mood, know that the lyrics often gently rib the narrator as he acknowledges his own foibles. Be on the lookout for audience favorites <cite class="short-work"><span class="h-card p-name">George Clooney</span></cite> and <cite class="short-work">Lonely Swedish Girl.</cite>

Originally a duo with Lauretti’s sister <span class="h-card p-name p-given-name">Jessica</span>, This Frontier Needs Heroes has always featured a rotating crew of supporting musicians. The band members are spread out across the U.S., so most shows are just Lauretti himself, with full-band treatment of his material mostly relegated to recording sessions.

Speaking of recordings, This Frontier Needs Heroes has released four full-length albums, most recently <time datetime="2016">2016</time>’s <cite><a href="https://amzn.to/2Ui9TdQ" target="_blank" rel="external noopener">Real Job</a></cite>. For fanatics, there’s also a <time datetime="2014">2014</time> CD single and a <a href="http://piaptk.limitedrun.com/products/503547-this-frontier-needs-heroes-2012-8-pyramid-shaped-pic-disc" target="_blank" rel="external noopener">very limited edition 2012 vinyl single</a>. Good luck tracking that last one down.

Lauretti opened <time datetime="2018-07-14T11:00:00-5:00">festival Saturday</time> with a solo set at <span class="h-card p-org">Lou’s Rocky Road Tavern</span>.
