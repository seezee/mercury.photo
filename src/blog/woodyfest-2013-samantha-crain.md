Critically praised[^1] Choctaw singer <span class="h-card p-name">Samantha Crain</span> appeared on the Crystal Stage at Woodyfest in <time datetime="2013-07-12T17:00-5:00">2013</time>. Songwriter and multi-instrumentalist <span class="h-card p-name">Kyle Reid</span>, singer/songwriter <span class="h-card p-name">John Calvin Abney</span>, and fiddler <span class="h-card p-name">Daniel Foulks</span> accompanied her. This was Crain’s fourth time performing at the festival.

[^1]: Crain has received glowing reviews in <span class="h-card p-org publication">Rolling Stone</span>, <span class="h-card p-org publication">SPIN</span>, <span class="h-card p-org publication">No Depression</span>, <span class="h-card p-org publication">National Public Radio</span>, <span class="h-card p-org publication">Public Radio International</span>,<span class="h-card p-org publication">The Guardian</span>, <span class="h-card p-org publication">New Musical Express</span>, <span class="h-card p-org publication">Uncut</span>, and other major media outlets.

A songwriter known for her broad range and eclectic style,[^2] Crain is also a music producer and poet. She won two <strong>Native American Music Awards</strong> in <time datetime="2009">2009</time>, one as a songwriter and one for best folk album. In <time datetime="2019">2019</time> her album <cite>You Had Me at Goodbye</cite> won the <strong>Indigenous Music Award</strong> for best rock album.

[^2]: Crain genre-hops effortlessly from folksy acoustic singer-songwriter to avant-garde and alternative rock.

A veteran touring musician, Crain has opened for <span class="h-card p-org">First Aid Kit</span>, <span class="h-card p-org">Neutral Milk Hotel</span>, <span class="h-card p-name">Lucy Rose</span>, <span class="h-card p-org">The Avett Brothers</span>, <span class="h-card p-org">The Mountain Goats</span>, <span class="h-card p-name">Brandi Carlile</span>, <span class="h-card p-name">Langhorne Slim</span>, and others.

Crain’s most recent Woodyfest appearance was on the main stage in <time datetime="2016-07-15T20:00-5:00">2016</time>. Her forthcoming album, <cite>A Small Death</cite>, is available for [pre-order](https://samantha-crain.lnk.to/ASmallDeath){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}.
