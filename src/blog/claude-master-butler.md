I met <span class="h-card p-name">Claude Butler</span> when he first moved to Norman in <time datetime="1987">1987</time>. He had an arrangement with the management at Sooner Fashion Mall (now called “Sooner Mall”) in which they let him set up a shoeshine stand rent-free near the mall’s center court. That arrangement ended in <time datetime="1990">1990</time> when some corporate bean-counters got wind of it and demanded that Claude either pay rent, or move out. But that was long enough for Claude to make some lasting friendships and loyal clients, including one with Heisman Trophy winner and local businessman <span class="h-card p-name">Steve Owens</span>, former University of Oklahoma football coach <span class="h-card p-name">Barry Switzer</span>, and Olympic medalist <span class="h-card p-name">Bart Connor</span>. Your humble chronicler counts himself as a Friend of the Master as well.

Claude and I really hit it off when we discovered a mutual love for mid-twentieth-century jazz, especially the small ensemble bop and post-bop jazz exemplified by <span class="h-card p-given-name">Miles</span>, <span class="h-card p-nickname">Trane</span>, <span class="h-card p-nickname">Dizzy</span>, <span class="h-card p-family-name">Monk</span>, <span class="h-card p-family-name">Mingus</span>, and their contemporaries. On occasion, we’ve settled in on my sofa to watch a motion picture biopic of one of our jazz heroes. I’ve complained to him about the one time I ever got to see Miles play, only to have the night spoiled by a couple of loud drunks in the audience. Claude dropped by <time datetime="2016-06-28">recently</time> to ask me for help re-wiring his loudspeakers, so I made us drinks and took this portrait. It was shot on a Wista 45DX field camera with x-ray film. For more on my use of x-ray film, see the [OKIE-X](/okie-x/) project page.

Claude may be found at his shoeshine stand on weekdays at the <span class="h-adr">Midway Grocery and Market, <span class="p-street-address">601 West Eufala Street</span> in <span class="p-locality">Norman</span>, <span class="p-region">Oklahoma</span></span>.
