My friend <span class="h-card p-name">David Goad</span> (<span class="h-card p-org">[Kali-Ra](/blog/david-goad-kali-ra-interview-gallery-pt-1/)</span>, <span class="h-card p-org">Of the Tower</span>) and his wife <span class="h-card p-name"><span class="p-given-name">Alicia</span></span> are heading to Europe for vacation soon. David needed a refresher course on driving a manual transmission vehicle, since the rental cars will have stick-shifts.

So I offered him driving lessons in my MINI last week. We tooled around in my neighborhood for a while before moving to the Lloyd Noble Center parking lot. David’s biggest trouble was forgetting to shift from first gear to second. I should note that he never, not once, ground my MINI's gears, so points for that.

After Lloyd Noble we took a supper break. Our first choice for dinner was closed by the time we got there, so we opted for pub grub at Blackbird Gastropub. While we were there I snapped this portrait. David and his band have a number of [shows scheduled]("https://www.facebook.com/officialkalira/events){target=_blank rel="external noopener"} before he departs for Europe, so be sure to check them out.
