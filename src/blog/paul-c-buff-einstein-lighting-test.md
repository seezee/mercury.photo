We bought a used Paul C. Buff “Einstein” (model E640) studio strobe just before Christmas; the package arrived on New Year’s Eve. So far we really like it; we’ve been trying different settings, modifiers, distances and so forth. We took this portrait with two lights: the Einstein, plus an inexpensive speedlight. Here’s the setup:

<dl>
  <dt>Subject distance from both lights</dt>
  <dd>&asymp; 8&prime;</dd>
  <dt>Key Light</dt>
  <dd>Einstein is to subject front & left, ~7&prime; up, with a 43&Prime; silver "bounce" <a target="_blank" rel="external noopener" href="https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B001OKBLEE/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=B001OKBLEE&linkCode=as2&tag=mercphotamz08-20&linkId=e8982bc2ff5e17fdb48dfa9e28133602">umbrella</a> angled at subject</dd>
  <dd>Einstein is set to 5600K at ƒ/-5.0</dd>
  <dt>Fill Light</dt>
  <dd><a target="_blank" rel="external noopener" href="https://amzn.to/2DBjDZl">Yongnuo YN 560 III</a> is to subject front & right, ~5&prime; up, with <a target="_blank" rel="external noopener" href="https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B007S02GX4/ref=as_li_tl?ie=UTF8&camp=1789&creative=9325&creativeASIN=B007S02GX4&linkCode=as2&tag=mercphotamz08-20&linkId=54844366f670ee57175ba9b9336f63dd">LumiQuest Softbox III</a> aimed at subject</dd>
  <dd>Yongnuo is set to <sup>1</sup>&frasl;<sub>32</sub> power & 24mm focal length</dd>
  <dt>Camera distance from subject</dt>
  <dd>&asymp; 1 meter (3&prime; 3&Prime;)</dd>
  <dt>Lens</dt>
  <dd>Leica Tele-Elmarit-M 90mm ƒ/2.8 Nr. 11 800</dd>
  <dt>ISO</dt>
  <dd>160</dd>
  <dt>Shutter speed</dt>
  <dd><sup>1</sup>&frasl;<sub>180</sub> second</dd>
  <dt>Aperture</dt>
  <dd>ƒ/2.8</dd>
</dl>

We made basic tonal adjustments on the image in Adobe Lightroom.
