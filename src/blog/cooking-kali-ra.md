Our good friend <span class="h-card p-name">David Goad</span> is [raising money](http://kalira.bandcamp.com/merch/tour-donation){target="_blank" rel="external noopener"} to tour with his band [Kali Ra](/blog/david-goad-kali-ra-interview-gallery-pt-1/) by recording a cooking show. He asked us to kick things off by talking about something we truly love: American whiskey (bourbon & rye). <span class="h-card p-name">Will Harrison</span> from <span class="h-card p-org">the [OKC Edge](http://www.theokcedge.com/){target="_blank" rel="external noopener"}</span> ran cameras and sound.

{% image "./src/assets/images/blog/2012-11-27-kali-ra-pt-1/czahller-20121013-23-42-04.jpg", "img-constrained", "David Goad performing at the Hi-Lo Club in Oklahoma City. The image is black & white.", "David (V)" %}</mpb-dialog-img>

We’re afraid we talked so long that we used up Will’s batteries and flash storage, so he had to finish the recording with his phone, which accounts for the shaky video near the end. Next time he'll know that David and I both love to gab.

The session featured these drinks:

* Old-fashioned cocktail
* Whiskey sour
* Sazerac
* Vieux carré
* Manhattan cocktail
* Whiskey julep, a.k.a. mint julep

Somehow, we managed not to be hungover the next day.
