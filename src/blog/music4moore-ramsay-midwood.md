## The Tornado

On <time datetime="2013-05-03T02:46-5:00">3 May 2013 at approximately 2:46 p.m.</time>, a tornado touched ground near Oklahoma City. The mile-wide twister would eventually travel twenty miles over a period of roughly forty minutes, becoming an <abbr title="Enhanced Fujita Scale">EF</abbr>5 tornado and leaving death and destruction in its wake as it devastated the suburb of Moore. The final death toll stood at twenty-four. Hundreds more were injured or left homeless.{.drop-cap}

## The Concert

Within a day or so of the storm’s passing, music promoter <span class="h-card p-name">Steven White</span> and local businessman [<span class="h-card p-name">Jonathan Fowler</span>](https://www.oudaily.com/l_and_a/arts_and_entertainment/local-music-makes-norman-resident-jonathan-fowler-s-heart-sing/article_400c43cc-b85f-5557-8275-1e3c365dc492.html){target=_blank rel="external noopener"} were organizing Music4Moore, a benefit concert for the tornado’s victims. Scores of local and regional musicians donated their time, along with local businesses and vendors as well as dozens of volunteer workers, with all proceeds going to the [Red Cross of Central and Western Oklahoma](https://www.redcross.org/ok/oklahoma-city){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}.

The organizers set up three stages: two stages for ticket-holders at the Bricktown Events Center, and an outdoor stage, free and open to the public. The outdoor stage later moved to the Wormy Dog Saloon due to inclement weather. The Queen of Rock ’n’ Roll [<span class="h-card p-name">Wanda Jackson</span>](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/wanda-jackson/id7036716?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"} headlined along with Broken Arrow native and critically acclaimed rhythm and blues revivalist [<span class="h-card p-name">J.D. McPherson</span>](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/jd-mcpherson/id398896732?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}. Also appearing were:

Texas singer-songwriter <span class="h-card p-name">Ramsay Midwood</span> traveled from Austin with his band to perform on the west stage, entertaining the audience with his rootsy Southern Gothic songs. Accompanying him were guitarist <span class="h-card p-name">Bill Mullins</span>, bassist <span class="h-card p-name">Jeff Johnston</span>, drummer <span class="h-card p-name">Scott Mason</span>, and special guest and Norman musician <span class="h-card p-name">Daniel Foulks</span> (<span class="h-card p-org">III<sup>rd</sup> Generation Bluegrass Band</span>, <span class="h-card p-name">Samantha Crain</span>) on fiddle.

When I bumped into Music4Moore organizer Steven White at the Gourds’ [Summer Breeze Concert Series](https://www.normandepot.org/summer-breeze){target=_blank rel="external noopener"} performance in Lions Park last week, he asked if I’d published the pictures from Ramsay’s set anywhere. I had to admit I hadn’t processed *any* photos from the benefit&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&mdash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;I shot over 750 pictures that night&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&mdash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;but that I’d let him know as soon as they were ready. So, even ’though I’d planned to deal with images of the headliners first, I changed my mind and worked on these instead. This is for you, Steven.
