Originally formed in 2011 by members of <span class="h-card p-org">Kali Ra</span>, [<span class="h-card p-org">Psychic Milk</span>](/blog/psychic-milk-at-the-hilo-club/), <span class="h-card p-org">the [Venditos](/blog/venditos-interview-gallery/)</span>, and a small constellation of musicians associated with <abbr title="Academy of Contemporary Music">ACM</abbr> at <abbr title="University of Central Oklahoma">UCO</abbr> for an extravagant performance of <span class="h-card p-org">Pink Floyd</span>’s <cite>[The Wall](https://music.apple.com/us/album/the-wall/1065975633?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</cite>, the <span class="h-card p-org">Surrogate Band</span> has reassembled for a series of <span class="h-card p-name">[David Bowie](https://itunes.apple.com/us/artist/david-bowie/id551695?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</span> tribute shows. Their final Oklahoma performance, featuring elaborate costumes, hair, and makeup reflecting the different periods in the chameleon-like singer’s career, is this <time datetime="2014-06-21T22:30-5:00">Saturday</time> at VZD’s Restaurant and Club in Oklahoma City.{.drop-cap}

<span class="h-card p-name">[David Goad](/blog/david-goad-kali-ra-interview-gallery-pt-1/)</span> (Kali Ra, <span class="h-card p-org">Of the Tower</span>) portrays Bowie, accompanied by guitarists <span class="h-card p-name">Justin Hogan</span> (<span class="h-card p-org">[Bowlsey](/blog/bowlsey-velvet-monkey-interview-gallery/)</span>, Psychic Milk) and <span class="h-card p-name">Chris Wilson</span> (guitar instructor at Maughan Studios), bassist <span class="h-card p-name">Gabriel Barham</span> (the Venditos, <span class="h-card p-org">Starcruisers</span>), keyboardist <span class="h-card p-name">Richie Zenner</span> (the Venditos, <span class="h-card p-org">the Planets Align</span>, and <span class="h-card p-org">Holloweye</span>), drummer <span class="h-card p-name">Chris Sanders</span> (the Venditos, the Planets Align), and saxophonist <span class="h-card p-name">Mike Mitchell</span> (<span class="h-card p-org">Captain Eyeball</span>). Rounding out the band are backing vocalists <span class="h-card p-name">J.R. Reagor</span> (who also provides utility percussion) and <span class="h-card p-name">Amy Downes</span>. Both singers also perform solo outside The Surrogate Band.

  <span class="h-card p-name">Kristine Kamen</span> manages the stage and provides key costumes and makeup, with additional hair and makeup provided by <span class="h-card p-org">Revel Eight Salon and Spa</span>.

  For the band’s <span class="h-card p-org">Hi-Lo Club</span> performance, ukelele player and singer <span class="h-card p-name">Michael “<span class="h-card p-nickname">Lumière</span>” Davis</span> provided entertainment during the mid-show break.
