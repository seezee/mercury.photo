Although singer-songwriter <span class="h-card p-name">[Audrey Auld](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/audrey-auld/id39868384?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</span> describes her style as <q>music with the dirt left on,</q> that’s dirt from a distinctly different hemisphere&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;—&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;the southern one.

Born in Australia and raised in the Tasmanian bush, she was exposed to music early on (her father played jazz piano and her stepfather was in a jazz band). Moving from classical violin in pre-adolescence to punk rock in her teens, she finally landed on country music as an adult, eventually releasing an EP, <cite>Audrey</cite>,[^1] with <span class="h-card p-name">Bill Chambers</span> in 1997.

[^1]: The EP has, sadly, gone out of print, but is available on the <cite>[Reckless Records Garage Sale](https://music.apple.com/us/album/reckless-records-garage-sale/id643534982?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</cite> compilation.

Auld currently lives in Nashville, performing and recording under her married name, Audrey Auld-Mezera. She has released over a dozen albums, with her latest, <cite>[Tonk](https://music.apple.com/us/album/tonk/id688094033?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</cite>, just out.
