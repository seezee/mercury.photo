# {{ title }}

<p>Prodigy’s Woodyfest Debut</p></stack-l></hgroup>

We first met singer and songwriter <span class="h-card p-name">Ken Pomeroy</span> on <time datetime="2015-12-21T22:35:59-6:00">New Year’s Eve, 2015</time>. Her father, <span class="h-card p-name p-given-name">Skippy</span>, builds racing motorcycles in the large shop behind the family home and occasionally hosts concerts on the small stage inside.[^1] <span class="h-card p-org"><span class="p-name">Kyle Reid</span> and the Low-Swinging Chariots</span> headlined the end-of-year celebration. Before Reid’s set, Pomeroy played a short set of covers and original songs to a receptive audience.

[^1]: We’ve been in many green rooms. But Skippy’s green room, by day a spray booth for painting motorcycles, is the nicest. Before a concert Skippy furnishes it with comfortable, upholstered furniture, tables, lamps, a rug, and artwork. And of course he and his wife <span class="h-card p-name p-given-name">Wendy</span> stock it with plenty of snacks and drinks for the musicians. The shop does triple-duty as it also sometimes serves as <a href="https://www.okgazette.com/oklahoma/hardship-inspired-wendy-thomas-pomeroys-first-photography-exhibit-in-nearly-five-years/Content?oid=2981202" target="_blank" rel="external noopener">Wendy’s photography studio</a>.
</div>

Besides the maturity of her writing, what really struck us about Pomeroy’s performance was that she had already developed a stage presence. Despite being only thirteen-years-old, Pomeroy did not stand motionless with her eyes closed, retreating into some performer’s inner sanctum. She kept her lids up. She feigned eye-contact[^2] with the audience. She talked to them. She *engaged* with them.

[^2]: We say “feigned” because the lighting at Skippy’s is pretty decent, which means it’s too bright for the performers to actually see the audience. That’s good, because it means the audience *can* see the performers.

    It also means musicians may be tempted to close their eyes for their own comfort even if shyness or stage-fright isn’t already an issue. And we know many grown-up men and women who haven’t figured out that *that* diminishes the fan experience because it puts up a barrier to *connecting with the audience*. We asked Pomeroy after the show who taught her to keep her eyes open like that. <q>I just figured it out,</q> she said.

Pomeroy began singing, writing songs, and playing the ukulele at age nine. By ten she was playing guitar. Since we saw her at Skippy’s she’s opened for or shared stages with <span class="h-card p-name">Wanda Jackson</span>, <span class="h-card p-name">Stony LaRue</span>, and <span class="h-card p-name">Bo Phillips</span>. She’s performed on the big stages at the <span class="h-card p-org">Centennial Opry</span> and the <span class="h-card p-org">Riverwind Casino</span>. She was the first winner of the <span class="h-card p-org"><span class="h-card p-name">Jimmy LaFave</span> Songwriting Contest</span> <time datetime="2018">last year</time> and is also a recipient of the Rising Star Award from the <span class="h-card p-org">Songwriters Association of Norman</span>. <cite>The Oklahoma Room at Folk Alliance <time datetime="2018">2018</time></cite> compilation[^3] included her song, <cite class="short-work">The Sidewalk Song,</cite> which she performed at <span class="h-card p-org">Folk Alliance International</span> <time datetime="2018-02">last February</time>.

[^3]: Tulsa’s non-profit music label <span class="h-card p-org">Horton Records</span> releases an annual compilation to benefit Okie artists showcasing at Folk Alliance International. Past issues, as well as Pomeroy’s new album, are available <a href="https://hortonrecords.bandcamp.com/" target="_blank" rel="external noopener">from their Bandcamp page</a>.

Pomeroy has been appearing on the unofficial stage at <span class="h-card p-org"><span class="h-card p-name">Lou</span>’s Rocky Road Tavern</span>[^4] since <time datetime="2016-07">2016</time>. <time datetime="2018-07-14T18:00:00-5:00">2018</time> marked her official Woodyfest début, where she performed selections from her first full-length album, <cite>Hallways</cite>. <time datetime="2018-01">Earlier in the year</time>, she played <span class="h-card p-org">Mile 0 Fest</span>. <time datetime="2019-02-01">At the time of this writing</time>, she is in Key West, Florida, making her second appearance at Mile 0.

[^4]: Lou’s Rocky Road Tavern has served as an unofficial venue for Woodyfest since its inception, featuring festival performers and hosting open-mic sessions throughout. <time datetime="2018">Last year</time> festival organizers bestowed them official status.
