It just keeps getting better for <span class="h-card p-name">[Dan Weber](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/dan-weber/id477234437?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</span>. In <time datetime="2010">2010</time>, he was nominated for the prestigious <span class="h-card p-name">Dave Carter</span> Songwriting Contest. Two years later, he was a finalist in the Kerrville Folk Festival “New Folk” competition. This year, he won third prize in the <span class="h-card p-name"><span class="p-nickname">Woody</span> <span class="p-family-name">Guthrie</span></span> Songwriting Contest, earning him a spot on the Crystal Theatre stage in Okemah.[^1]

[^1]: Home to the annual Woody Guthrie Folk Festival.

His new CD <cite>[Ash and Bone](https://music.apple.com/us/album/ash-and-bone/id499666252?uo=4&at=10ldhk){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</cite> has garnered positive acclaim from <cite class="h-card p-org">American Songwriter Magazine</cite> and drawn the attention of the <span class="h-card p-org">Great American Songwriting Contest</span> and the <span class="h-card p-org">Great Lakes Songwriting Contest</span>.

Weber’s even better performing live, where he exudes a genuine “aw shucks” air. You can tell he’s glad to be wherever he is, anytime&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&mdash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;just ask the folks at the campground, whom he serenaded when he dropped by their campfire song-circles over the course of the festival. Be sure to ask him for a “What would <span class="h-card p-name"><span class="p-given-name">Hank</span></span> and <span class="h-card p-name"><span class="p-given-name">Jesus</span></span> Do?” bumper sticker the next time you see him.
