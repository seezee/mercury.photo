## Oklahoma Feel Good Music

Contrary to their name, the band <span class="h-card p-org">And Then There Were Two</span> does not comprise two members. The band numbers three: singer <span class="h-card p-name">Amy Carlin Lee</span>, singer and guitarist <span class="h-card p-name">Austin <abbr class="noscaps">K.</abbr> Haworth</span>, and percussionist <span class="h-card p-name">Chad Varnell</span>. On occasion the band expands by a member or two, but these three are the band’s founders and nucleus.

Their press kit describes their sound as <q>harmony based and lyrically driven</q> music that is <q>familiar [and] danceable</q> and emphasizes <q>catchy storytelling.</q> They call it <q>Oklahoma Feel Good Music.</q>[^1]

[^1]: <cite>[And Then There Were Two Press Kit](https://docs.wixstatic.com/ugd/c4f403_86e687e9a8c84dd78ba1a747b701b38c.odt){target=_blank rel="external noopener"}</cite>, retrieved from https&colon;//www<wbr>.<wbr>and<wbr>then<wbr>there<wbr>were<wbr>two<wbr>music<wbr>.<wbr>com/ <time datetime="2019-01-13">13 January 2019</time>. The website has since gone offline.

And Then There Were Two formed after Lee and Haworth were introduced by a mutual friend; Haworth’s long-time friend Varnell soon joined them.[^2]

[^2]: <cite>Ibid.</cite>

Lee’s mother was a singer and her father also loved and instilled a love of music in her. She joined her church choir at age five and performed her first solo at age seven. She graduated from college with a musical theatre degree.[^3]

[^3]: And Then There Were Two website <cite>About</cite> page, retrieved from https&colon;//www<wbr>.<wbr>oklahoma<wbr>feel<wbr>goodmusic<wbr>.<wbr>com/about/ <time datetime="2019-01-13">13 January 2019</time>.

Haworth’s father handed him his <time datetime="1975">1975</time> Alvarez Hummingbird guitar when he was fourteen. In the ensuing years, he formed numerous original bands, hosted open mics, and wrote prodigiously.[^4]

[^4]: <cite>Ibid.</cite>

Like Lee, Varnell’s mother was musical (she was an accomplished pianist). And like Lee, he took up music in the church&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;&mdash;&NoBreak;&hairsp;&NoBreak;he began drumming for worship service as a third grader.[^5]

[^5]: <cite>Ibid.</cite>

Lee, Haworth, and Varnell were accompanied during their Woodyfest showcase by <span class="h-card p-name">Nellie Clay</span>&rsquo;s bassist, <span class="h-card p-name">Luke Mullinex</span>.
