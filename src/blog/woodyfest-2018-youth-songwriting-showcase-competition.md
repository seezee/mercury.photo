Woodyfest held its inaugural Woodyfest Youth Songwriting/Showcase Competition in <time datetime="2018">2018</time>. The showcase featured performers age sixteen or younger. Master of Ceremonies and regular Woodyfest performer <span class="h-card p-name">Lauren Lee</span> announced the winners during an awards ceremony on <time datetime="2018-07-14T13:00:00-5:00">Saturday afternoon</time> from the Hen House stage.

Third prize went to <span class="h-card p-name">Autie Rich</span> for her cover of <span class="h-card p-name"><span class="p-nickname">Woody</span> Guthrie</span>’s <cite class="short-work">Minor Key.</cite> <span class="h-card p-name">Kelby Vass</span> took second prize for his cover of the <span class="h-card p-name">James Taylor</span> classic <cite class="short-work">Fire and Rain.</cite> First place winner <span class="h-card p-name">Riley Amanda</span> played her original song <cite class="short-work">Rich and Poor.</cite>

Each of the winners received cash and other prizes provided by local sponsors. Additionally, the first place winner received a VIP pass to the Pastures of Plenty on <time datetime="2018-07-14T19:00:00-5:00">Saturday night</time>, where Ms. Riley showcased her winning song. The other five contestants were <span class="h-card p-name">Hannah Carriker</span>, <span class="h-card p-name">Amour Carriker</span>, <span class="h-card p-name">Lillian Hill</span>, <span class="h-card p-name">London Reilly</span>, and <span class="h-card p-name">Blakelee Pugh</span>.

The showcase was originally conceived as a songwriting competition. Organizers decided to allow cover songs to give more kids an opportunity to play in front of an audience. The competition is part of “Woody’s Kids,” a youth-focused program entering its third year. The program also features songwriting workshops; <time datetime="2019">this year</time> the number of workshops will double from two to four.
